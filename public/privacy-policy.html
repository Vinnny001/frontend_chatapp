Privacy Policy

ChatApp · Effective date: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ChatApp handles information when you use our mobile application and website. ChatApp is operated by JUJATECH (“we,” “us,” or “our”). Please replace the bracketed operator and contact details below with accurate information before publishing this policy.

Information we handle

  • Account information: your name, email address, phone number, username (if you choose one), optional profile details, and information needed to create and secure your account. We also handle login credentials in a protected form and account/device session information.
  • Messages and shared content: messages, group and profile information, reactions, polls, status updates, reports, and the photos, videos, voice notes, documents, or other content you choose to send. This may include information about when messages are sent, delivered, or read and which devices or conversations are involved.
  • Contacts, if you grant permission: ChatApp reads names and phone numbers from your device’s address book to identify contacts who use ChatApp and help you invite others. Phone numbers are sent to our service for matching. Contact names and match results are stored on your device; if you use linked devices, contact-match information may be synchronized between your devices through the app’s encrypted device-sync feature. You can deny or revoke contacts permission in your device settings.
  • Calls and device permissions: if you use voice or video calling, the app accesses your microphone and, for video, your camera after you grant permission. Call audio/video is transmitted to other call participants and may pass through communication relays or call-service infrastructure to establish the call. The app does not need your location or contacts permission to make a call.
  • Device and service information: device/session identifiers, push notification tokens, app and operating-system information, connection and diagnostic data, and server logs such as IP address and request time may be processed to provide, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.
  • Information you send to support: the details and attachments you choose to provide when contacting us or submitting a report.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and manage accounts, authenticate users, and protect account access;
  • send and synchronize messages, calls, status updates, files, and notifications;
  • match contacts who use ChatApp and enable invitations when you request them;
  • operate linked devices, prevent abuse, respond to reports, and provide support; and
  • maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve ChatApp and meet legal obligations.

We do not use the app to serve third-party advertisements. The app’s current client does not include an advertising or analytics SDK; infrastructure providers may still process connection and diagnostic information as described in this policy.

Encryption and local storage

ChatApp supports end-to-end encrypted messaging for conversations that have completed the app’s encryption setup. In those conversations, message content is encrypted on the sending device and is intended to be readable by the conversation participants’ devices. Attachments sent using the encrypted attachment feature are encrypted on the sending device before upload. Some older messages may not be end-to-end encrypted. Information such as account details, delivery and presence metadata, and information needed to operate the service may still be processed by our systems.

Chat history, encryption keys, and downloaded media may be stored locally on your device so you can use the app offline. Signing out removes the app’s local encryption data and account session on that device; it does not by itself delete your account or information held by the service.

When information is shared

We share information only as needed for the purposes described here:

  • With other users: information and content you choose to share in a conversation or group is available to its participants. Profile information may also be visible to people who can find or contact you through the service.
  • With service providers: our API, hosting, file-storage, email-delivery, real-time messaging, and call-service providers process information for us to operate ChatApp. These providers are authorized to process it for the services they provide and subject to their applicable terms and safeguards. Group calls may use LiveKit; Android push notifications use Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google). Email providers deliver verification and account-recovery messages.
  • For safety or legal reasons: we may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect users, investigate abuse, or protect our rights and the security of the service.
  • Business changes: information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or transfer of the service, subject to appropriate protections.

We do not sell your personal information.
